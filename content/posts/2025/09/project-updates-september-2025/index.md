# Account services
I have spent the past two weeks working almost exclusively on the account services for most of 6 days a week. This means, that they will likely also already be deployed by the time you read this. I have moved the account services to a new domain, as I have split up the backend and frontend of the account services. They can now be found [here](https://account.janishutz.com). The store and MusicPlayer obviously also required a small update to accommodate the changes made to the account system.

The new account services have many additional security features and a much better looking UI that is more closely following the design the store and now also my website have, which in my mind is a large improvement. 

I have also added automated account deletion, retained TOTP and Email-based Two Factor Authentication, but I have added backup codes as I have massively improved encryption of the account data, which makes it super hard and annoying for me to help you with account recovery manually.

And, you will stay logged in for longer and I have added numerous protections to the system, which should mostly stay out of your way
